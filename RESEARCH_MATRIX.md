## Promotion rule

API, SDK, CCXT, Hummingbot or MCP availability alone never permits reward-seeking automation. `api_reward_eligible=true` requires a current official Program/Rewards/Points/XP source explicitly establishing API-originated qualifying activity. Current explicit positives: **Pacifica, Hibachi, GRVT**.

## Japan rule

Japan being absent from a prohibited-jurisdiction list is insufficient for `LEGAL_STATUS=PASS`. Perpetual/derivative access, crypto-service regulation, registration/warning-list status and current Terms require explicit review. Until that review is complete, all targets remain `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and LIVE is blocked.
